Why This System?

Built specifically for academic rigor, transparent student voice, and institutional improvement.

100% Student Anonymity

Zero student IDs, roll numbers, or personal identifying information is tracked. Students provide candid, constructive feedback without fear of bias or retribution.

Multi-Dimensional Analysis

Evaluates faculty across 5 distinct academic criteria: Concept Clarity, Syllabus Coverage, Punctuality & Communication, Doubt Solving Mentorship, and Lab Guidance.

Pandas, NumPy & Matplotlib

Backend statistical computations leverage NumPy for standard deviations and distributions, Pandas for grouped aggregations, and Matplotlib for publication-ready visual charts.

College Project Evaluation Ready

Includes automated unit tests with pytest, MySQL relational schema, sample database seeder, and viva presentation documentation.
